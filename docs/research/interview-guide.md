# Interview Guide (30–40 min)

Rules: ask about **past behaviour**, not opinions about a hypothetical product.
Don't pitch MedFlow until the last 5 minutes. Record numbers whenever possible.
Never collect patient information.

## Context (5 min)
1. Hospital size (beds), departments, how many stores/sub-stores?
2. Your role and how long you've done it. Who else touches inventory?
3. What system do you record stock in today? (HIS module, ERP, Tally, Excel, paper)

## Last stockout (10 min)
4. Tell me about the last time an item ran out. What item? How did you find out?
5. What did it cost — emergency purchase, delayed procedure, staff time?
6. How often does that happen in a month?

## Ordering (8 min)
7. Walk me through how you decided the last purchase order. Who decided the quantity?
8. How do you choose a supplier? Where do you keep price / lead time information?
9. Which supplier is least reliable? How do you know?

## Expiry & accuracy (5 min)
10. When did you last write off expired stock? Roughly how much?
11. How often does system stock match physical count? How do you reconcile?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
12. If you could fix one thing about supplies tomorrow, what would it be?
13. (Now show V1 demo) What's missing? What would you never use?
14. Who else should I talk to?
