## Validation targets (exit criteria for V0)

- [ ] 5+ interviews with procurement / store / inventory staff at 50–300 bed hospitals
- [ ] 2+ interviews with distributors / suppliers
- [ ] Current workflow for one department mapped from a real hospital (not from this draft)
- [ ] One consumable category chosen with evidence (frequency of stockouts × cost of stockout)
- [ ] Current software documented (HIS/ERP module, Excel, Tally, paper registers)
- [ ] Top 3 pain points ranked by interviewees, not by us

## Chosen beachhead (provisional)

**City:** Bengaluru · **Segment:** 50–300 bed multispecialty hospitals ·
**Department:** Orthopaedics + General Surgery (OT consumables) ·
**Category:** surgical consumables (gloves, sutures, drapes, syringes, IV sets, dressings).

Reason: OT consumables are high-volume, procedure-linked (good for V2+ forecasting),
and an OT cancellation due to a missing item is a visible, costly failure.
**Revisit after interviews.**
